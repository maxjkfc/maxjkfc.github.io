Why AI-native

AI 工具人人都在用。差別在兩件事：有沒有一套能穩定交付正式環境的協作系統，以及有沒有足夠的工程判斷把關它的輸出。

Everyone uses AI tools. The difference comes down to two things: whether you have a collaboration system that ships to production reliably, and whether you have enough engineering judgment to vet its output.

0112 年工程判斷 × AI 槓桿

AI 寫得快，對不對要有人看得出來。我看得出來，因為這些坑 12 年裡都踩過：架構決策、高併發交易、資料一致性。AI 放大的是資歷，不是取代它。

02Multi-agent 開發鏈，持續交付

把規格、實作、review 與驗證串成 multi-agent 開發鏈（Claude／Gemini／Codex）。實作由 AI 協作完成，架構決策與品質把關由我負責。已有功能持續交付正式環境。

03把營運流程教給 AI

一項人工營運作業原本要 3 小時，教給 AI 之後剩 10 分鐘；查帳流程也完整交接，每天自動比對兩邊資料庫的落差。

04自建 harness，不只是用工具

個人 LLM 知識庫管線、跨機 agent 記憶系統、Skill 沉澱，都是每天在用的第一手實作。模型會一直換，方法留在我身上，換一個模型只是換一個學生。

05可複製到整個團隊

工程文化我從零建過兩次：CI/CD、回顧會議、OKR、Event Storming。AI 工作流持續運作。這兩件事加起來，就能把一個團隊帶進 AI-native，不只我自己快。

0112 years of judgment × AI leverage

AI writes fast; someone has to be able to tell whether it's right. I can, because I've fallen into these pits over 12 years: architecture decisions, high-concurrency trading, data consistency. AI amplifies experience—it doesn't replace it.

02Multi-agent dev chain, shipping continuously

Spec, implementation, review and verification strung into a multi-agent dev chain (Claude / Gemini / Codex). AI collaborates on the implementation; I own the architecture decisions and quality gate. Features already ship to production continuously.

03Teaching operations to AI

A manual ops task that took 3 hours drops to 10 minutes once taught to AI; reconciliation is fully handed over too, diffing the two databases automatically every day.

04Building the harness, not just using tools

A personal LLM knowledge-base pipeline, a cross-machine agent memory system, distilled Skills—all first-hand work I use daily. Models keep changing; the method stays with me. A new model is just a new student.

05Replicable across a whole team

I've built engineering culture from zero twice: CI/CD, retrospectives, OKRs, Event Storming. The AI workflow runs continuously. Together, those two things bring a team into AI-native—not just make me faster.

「把工作都教給 AI，那我的價值是什麼？」

"If you teach all the work to AI, what's your value?"

AI 可以接手重複性的工作，但決定什麼值得做、怎樣才算做對，以及遇到例外時如何處理，仍需要人的判斷。我把這些判斷轉化成規格、驗收標準與工作流程，讓 AI 能穩定執行，最後由我為上線結果負責。當一個流程被系統化，我就能把注意力移到下一個更值得解決的問題。

AI can take over the repetitive work, but deciding what's worth doing, what counts as doing it right, and how to handle the exceptions still needs human judgment. I turn that judgment into specs, acceptance criteria and workflows so AI can execute reliably—and I answer for what ships. Once a process is systematized, I move my attention to the next problem worth solving.

每個技術選型都先問：
「這解決的是現在的問題，
還是想像中的未來？」

Every tech choice starts with one question:
"Does this solve a problem I have now,
or an imagined future?"
